`webcrawler` is a compact crawler written in Go for discovering and traversing pages across the web. It reflects the lower-level foundations behind search and indexing systems, where fetching, parsing, and traversal logic all need to work together reliably.

I’d position it as a systems-oriented search project: simple in scope, but directly relevant to how larger retrieval pipelines start with content acquisition and structured page exploration.
