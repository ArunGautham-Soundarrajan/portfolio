[View on GitHub](https://github.com/ArunGautham-Soundarrajan/GoShare)

`GoShare` is a peer-to-peer file sharing system built in Go. The project explores direct node-to-node communication and the mechanics involved in moving files across a distributed network without relying on a single central transfer path.

It is a good example of hands-on distributed systems work, covering concerns such as transport, coordination, and the practical shape of a lightweight file sharing protocol.
