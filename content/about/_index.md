I’m **Arun**, a Lead AI Engineer at **Aviva**, where I work on building and deploying real-world AI systems.

A lot of my work involves taking ideas that sound good in theory and figuring out how to make them actually work in production. That usually means dealing with messy data, unclear requirements, and systems that don’t behave the way you expect. I enjoy that part more than I probably should.

## My Interests

Most of my recent work has been around building end-to-end AI systems, things like automated data ingestion pipelines for Search applications, backend services for real-time applications, and extracting useful signals from messy conversational data.

I’ve worked on systems that support live applications, where reliability and performance matter, and on APIs that other teams depend on. I enjoy building these kinds of systems because they force you to think beyond models and into how everything fits together in practice.

Lately, I’ve been getting more interested in retrieval and search systems. While I haven’t had the chance to go deep into tuning these systems at work, I’ve been exploring them more seriously on my own. I find the problem space really interesting, especially how small decisions in retrieval and ranking can significantly affect the quality of results.

I’m particularly drawn to problems where data is messy, requirements are unclear, and the system has to make useful decisions anyway.

## Beyond work

Outside of work, I experiment with systems in my **homelab**, exploring self-hosting, containers, and infrastructure. I also write about what I learn around ML, AI applications, and building practical solutions.
